# Swing pullback watchlist

Holding period: several days to a few weeks. Discover 38.2%–61.8% retracements and highlight 50%–61.8%, independent of fundamental/trade eligibility. Daily anchors use the latest confirmed two-bar pivot high over 90 completed sessions, with the lowest confirmed pivot low 5–60 bars earlier and a rise of at least two ATR. Both dates and the method are exposed. This is a proposed deterministic selection method, not a validated profitable strategy.

Swing entry checks now require that band, an upward 20/50-day trend, configured relative volume, a completed bullish close above the prior bar high and current-price hold, intact support, bounded stop risk and historical resistance targets meeting configured reward/risk (minimum 2:1). Existing demand confirmation, fundamental, earnings, quote freshness, cash-secured put liquidity/risk and notification controls remain in effect. Intrinsic valuation remains a separate assessment. Golden-zone membership alone never generates a trade or alert.

The rules-version migration invalidates old scan/review results while preserving user settings, valuations, credentials and journal. A fresh market-session scan is required after deployment. The new watchlist is limited to the configured universe and timestamped provider coverage; it is not an exhaustive market scan. No trades are placed.
